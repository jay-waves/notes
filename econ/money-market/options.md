
| | Call (看涨) | Put (看跌) |
--|------|------
Buyer | Right to buy X of stock at  \$Y | Right to sell X of stock at \$Y 
Seller (Writer) | Obligation to sell X of stock at \$Y | Obligation to buy X of stock at \$Y

* Y : Strike price / Exercise price of option buyer
* *Premium*: amount paid by buyer to the option writer
* *Exercise*: Process of deciding and advising option seller of intention to 
    exercise the right under the option

Premium = Intrinsic value + Time value
* Intrinsic Value: differnece between the underlying market price (S) and option strike prices (X)
* Time value: risk that the option will move in the money before expiry

Moneyness of Option | Call | Put | Remarks 
----|----|---|----
In-the-money (ITM)[^1] | X < S | X > S | Intrinsic Value > 0
At-the-money (ATM)[^2] | X = S | X = S | Intrisnsic Value = 0
Out-of-the-money (OTM) | X > S | X < S | Intrinsic Value = 0
Intrinsic Value | Max (0, S-X) | Max(0, X-S) 

[^1]: likely the option will be exercised

[^2]: it is break-even
