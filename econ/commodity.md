
## Commdities & their benchmarks

Engery and inflation:
* Crude Oil. --> Brent (Global traded seaborne crude). WTI (US)
* Natural Gas. --> Henry Hub (US). TTF (Eurpoe).
* Coal --> Newcastle (seaborne thermal coal in Asis-Pacific)

Economic growth:
* Copper --> LME Copper
* Iron Ore --> 62% Fe CFR China
* Aluminum --> LME Aluminum 

Financial uncertainty:
* Gold --> LBMA Gold Price

Food security:
* Wheat --> CBOT Wheat
* Corn & Soybeans --> CBOT
* Fertilizers  

You should look at what the country imports and exports.
